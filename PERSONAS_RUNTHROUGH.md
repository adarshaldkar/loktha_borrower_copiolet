## 1. Persona 1: Priya (29, Bengaluru · Salaried Software Engineer)

### A. Borrower Profile Inputs
* **Age**: 29
* **Employment Type**: Salaried MNC (5 years with current employer)
* **Monthly Take-Home**: ₹1,10,000
* **Existing Debt**: ₹14,000 car loan (2 years remaining)
* **Essential Living Costs**: ₹28,000 rent
* **Credit Score**: 780 (Prime 750+)
* **Loan Purpose**: Wedding / Lifestyle (Non-Productive)
* **Requested Amount**: ₹8,00,000

### B. Questions Asked by the Copilot
* **Tier 1 (Must Questions)**: Purpose, Target Amount (₹8.0L), Employment Type (Salaried MNC), Net Pay (₹1.10L), Existing EMIs (₹14,000), Rent/Living Costs (₹28,000), Age (29), Credit Score (780).
* **Tier 2 (Adaptive Salaried Branch)**: Years at Current Employer (5 years), Corporate Employer Tier (Top-Tier MNC).

### C. The 4 Outputs Produced
1. **O1 Verdict**: **`BORROW LESS`**
   * *Headline*: ⚖️ Borrow Less — Cap Loan to Safe Budget Ceiling
   * *Rationale*: Wedding is a non-productive lifestyle expense. A ₹8.0L loan creates ~₹26,400 EMI, pushing total monthly debt to ₹40,400 (36.7% FOIR) and consuming over 50% of monthly discretionary surplus.
2. **O2 Capacity**:
   * *Estimated Lender-Side Capacity*: **₹16,50,000** (using 50% FOIR without rent deduction).
   * *Safe Borrower Capacity*: **₹5,20,000** (preserves ₹33,000/mo cash buffer).
   * *Guidance*: "A bank may approve ₹16.5L, but your safe carry ceiling is ₹5.2L. Limit your borrowing to ₹5.2L."
3. **O3 Fair Rate & All-in APR**:
   * *Product Pathway*: Unsecured Personal Loan
   * *Fair Rate Band*: **10.75% – 11.75%** (Prime rate with 5-year corporate stability discount)
   * *Target Fair Rate*: **11.25%**
   * *All-in APR*: **11.95% APR** (1.5% processing fee + 18% GST)
4. **O4 Safe Monthly Outflow & Stress Test**:
   * *Safe Monthly EMI Ceiling*: **₹18,000 / month**
   * *Tenure Schedule (for ₹5.2L)*: 24m (₹24,300/mo) | 36m (**₹17,100/mo [Recommended]**) | 60m (₹11,400/mo)
   * *Stress Test*: -20% Income Drop leaves ₹31,000 surplus (✓ Resilient). +200 bps Rate Spike adds ₹500/mo (✓ Resilient).

### D. Priya's Negotiation Card Script
> *"I am seeking a sanction of ₹5,20,000. My credit score is 780 with 5 years at a Tier-1 MNC. Competing banks offer prime borrowers all-in rates between 10.75% and 11.75%.
